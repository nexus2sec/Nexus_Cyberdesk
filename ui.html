    WhatsApp Hijack SOS

    1. Immediately call close contacts: do not send money from my account.
    2. Re-register WhatsApp using your number and OTP.
    3. Enable 2-step verification and logout unknown linked devices.
    4. Report incident to 1930 and cybercrime portal.

    UPI Fraud SOP

    1. Disable UPI temporarily in bank app if available.
    2. Call 1930 and note complaint reference.
    3. Report to bank through grievance email and branch.
    4. Keep screenshots, transaction IDs, SMS alerts, and call logs.

    Community Awareness Kit

    Fake KYC links

    Never click unknown KYC links. Verify only from official app/site.

    QR receive scam

    Scanning QR and entering PIN means money out, not money in.

    Screen share trap

    Do not install remote apps from unknown support calls.

    This platform is for social assistance and first-response guidance. For legal and account action, always contact 1930, cybercrime portal, and your bank.
